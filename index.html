Each course is a single self-contained page — ten stages from vowels to literature, with the source language as the only metalanguage. No English tab labels, no Roman-only transliteration crutches. Just the letters, the words, and the bridges between two families of thought.

The courses follow a hand-crafted Bengali→Kannada reference: vowel families, consonant grids with “you already know this” notes, aside boxes with pair-specific insights, and literary bridges from writers you know to writers you don’t yet.
